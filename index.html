Behind the camera

Hi, I'm Kelvin.

I'm a Grandville-based filmmaker with a degree in Digital Cinematography from Full Sail University. Before I started Kelvin Film Co., I spent years building training and onboarding programs, so I know how to tell a story that's clear and doesn't just look pretty.

The name comes from the way cinematographers measure light. Every moment has its own color, from candlelit vows to a bright summer reception, and my job is to capture it as it really felt.

Bilingual English / Español. Your family hears their story in their own language.
