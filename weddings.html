Pregunta por tu fecha

Questions couples ask

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 9–12 months out. Summer and fall Saturdays go first. A 30% non-refundable retainer holds your date, and the balance is due before the wedding.

Do you travel outside Grand Rapids?

Yes. Travel within 50 miles of Grand Rapids is included, and I film anywhere in Michigan and beyond.

When will we get our film?

You'll get a sneak peek within 7 days and your full film within 8 weeks.

Can you work alongside our photographer?

Always. I coordinate with your photographer before the day so we never get in each other's shots.

Are you insured?

Yes. Kelvin Film Co. LLC carries liability insurance and can send a certificate to your venue.

Is your date still open?

Send your date and venue, and I'll let you know within 24 hours.
